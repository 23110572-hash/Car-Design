01 · Front design

        
A face built around the crash zone.

        
The 790 mm front overhang carries the 110 kW motor and the crash structure. Slim wrap-around headlamps run into a gloss-black grille with the Suzuki emblem, and vertical light signatures frame the corner intakes.

02 · Side profile

        
4.25 metres. Every millimetre has a job.

        
790 mm for the motor and crash zone, a 2,650 mm wheelbase for the floor battery and rear legroom, 810 mm for a 400 L boot.

03 · Rear design

        
A locked 400 L boot, and a port that fills it with range.

        
The 810 mm rear overhang holds a 400-litre boot: two large suitcases, locked away. On the left rear quarter, a flap opens onto the CCS2 charging port.

        
400 LBoot, locked
~24 min10→80% on a 120 kW DC charger
11 kWAC on-board charger
7.4 kWHome charger in the box · 10→100% in 6.9 h

        
Connected LED tail lamps, black garnish and the MARUTI SUZUKI plate follow the reference rear view.
